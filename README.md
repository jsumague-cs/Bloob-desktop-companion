# 🫐 Bloob – Desktop AI Companion

## Overview

**Bloob** is a desktop AI companion designed to make productivity feel encouraging rather than overwhelming.

Many productivity applications focus on deadlines, statistics, and constant notifications, which can leave users feeling stressed or guilty when they fall behind. Bloob takes a different approach by combining intelligent reminders, conversational AI, and a friendly desktop companion into one cohesive experience.

Instead of acting like a strict task manager, Bloob acts as a supportive companion that celebrates progress, encourages healthy habits, and helps users take the next small step toward their goals.

---

## Meet Blooberry

Bloob's companion is **Blooberry**, a curious little alien who traveled across the universe to understand how humans live.

During his journey, he discovered that people don't usually fail because they lack ability—they often struggle because life becomes overwhelming, motivation fades, or tasks feel too big to start.

Wanting to help, Blooberry chose to stay on Earth and accompany one human at a time, offering gentle encouragement, celebrating small victories, and reminding them that progress is made one step at a time.

Blooberry isn't here to pressure users into being productive.

He's here to remind them that every small step counts.

---

## Core Principles

* 🌱 Encourage, never shame.
* 🎉 Celebrate progress, no matter how small.
* 💙 Build consistency instead of perfection.
* 🔒 Respect user privacy.
* 🤝 Be a companion before being an assistant.
